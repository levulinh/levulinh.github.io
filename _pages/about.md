Hello, I'm Drew. Welcome to MTBox, my blog and workshop for AI systems, engineering notes, and the occasional life detour.

**Current work.** I'm an AI Product Squad Leader and senior AI/ML engineer based in Seoul, Korea. I build production AI systems, with a current focus on agentic workflows, LLM orchestration, RAG, multimodal reasoning, VLM/OCR, and evaluation frameworks.

**What I like building.** At Elice Group, I lead AI engineering work that turns research-grade ideas into products people can actually use. I like the part of engineering where models, product judgment, and real-world constraints all meet.

**Before Elice.** I earned my M.S. in Electrical Engineering from KAIST and worked on clinical-grade sleep AI at Asleep Inc, where I shipped deep learning models and collaborated on peer-reviewed medical AI research.

Outside work, I speak English, Vietnamese, and a little Korean. I love rock and Coldplay, and I am also a Hylian and a resident of house Ravenclaw.

On this blog, I share thoughts, experiments, and build notes around AI, coding, product engineering, photography, and gaming. I hope it gives you something useful to think with, whether you are building agents, tuning models, or wandering through the strange new software landscape.

Join me as we explore the fascinating realms between artificial intelligence and reality.
